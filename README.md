💎 Jewellery Stock Management System

Smart Inventory • Secure Management • Better Business

Jewellery Stock Management System is a modern, intelligent, and user-friendly web application designed to simplify and automate inventory management for jewellery businesses.

The system helps jewellers efficiently manage gold, silver, diamond, and gemstone jewellery, track product quantities, monitor stock values, manage purchases and sales, and calculate profits.

✨ Key Features

📦 1. Inventory Management

- Add, update, search, and delete jewellery products.
- Track available stock in real time.
- Organize jewellery by category, purity, and design.
- Assign unique product IDs.

🥇 2. Gold & Silver Management

- Track 24K, 22K, 18K, and other gold purity levels.
- Manage silver jewellery and utensils.
- Record gross weight, net weight, and stone weight.
- Calculate jewellery values using metal rates.

💰 3. Sales & Billing

- Generate customer invoices.
- Calculate making charges and wastage.
- Apply applicable GST automatically.
- Track daily, weekly, and monthly sales.

📊 4. Business Analytics

- Monitor total inventory value.
- Calculate revenue and profit.
- Identify best-selling jewellery.
- Analyze stock movement and business performance.

🔔 5. Smart Stock Alerts

- Receive low-stock notifications.
- Identify out-of-stock products.
- Track slow-moving inventory.
- Get reminders for stock replenishment.

👥 6. Customer & Supplier Management

- Maintain customer purchase records.
- Manage supplier information.
- Track purchases, payments, and outstanding balances.

🔐 7. Security & Data Management

- Secure administrator login.
- Role-based access for employees.
- Maintain transaction history.
- Protect important inventory records.

🎯 Our Mission

To empower jewellery businesses with smart digital tools that reduce manual work, improve inventory accuracy, minimize losses, and support profitable business decisions.
